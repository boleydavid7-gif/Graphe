# Graphe project governance

Graphe is currently maintained by its original project owner. It is being prepared as a public open-source project but has not yet completed the release review.

- **Decision-making:** The maintainer reviews and merges contributions. Design changes that affect document integrity, the native model, or licensing need documented review.
- **Development:** Proposals and bugs should be discussed through issues and pull requests after the public launch. The [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains validation expectations.
- **Licensing:** Original covered source is intended for MPL 2.0 publication after the clearance gates. Contributors retain the copyrights they own in their original work and license those contributions under the project's terms. Third-party content stays under its own terms.
- **Brand:** Public availability of source does not permit a fork to present itself as the official Graphe or ProairetOS product. See [trademark guidance](TRADEMARKS.md).
- **ProairetOS:** The broader operating-system application is a distinct product and is not included in the Graphe open-source license.
- **Releases and security:** No public release, supported-version promise, or maintained security-response deadline has been established. Release decisions depend on testing and [audit clearance](docs/OPEN_SOURCE_RELEASE.md).

Governance may evolve if a contributor community forms; changes should be documented publicly once the project launches.
